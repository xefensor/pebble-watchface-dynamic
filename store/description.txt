An animated clock with two Pebble readings and compatible activities.

See your chosen readings together, with matching numeric sizes that keep complete values and units. Compatible activities can show a primary item and an optional secondary summary. Music shows track, artist and available artwork; a loading symbol keeps the artwork position stable. Notifications are optional.

Choose Small, Medium, Large or Follow Pebble text, your clock format and palettes. Track changes slide the old item out as the new item enters. The face responds to Timeline Peek.

Requires the current Pebble mobile app with Plugins / permissions preview enabled and the permissions of your selected sources. Music and notification integration require Android. Providers control availability, units and original expiry. No developer weather server, account, advertisements or analytics.

Free. For Pebble Time 2 and Round 2. Round 2 has been validated in the emulator.
