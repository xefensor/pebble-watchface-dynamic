An animated Pebble watchface that keeps the time, your chosen readings and ongoing updates together.

Dynamic gives the clock a clear place at the top, with your chosen readings beneath it. See weather, battery or other information from compatible Pebble sources, then use the space below for what is happening now.

- Choose Columns, Stacked rows or Four when idle. The four-reading grid keeps the selected pair when an activity or Timeline Peek needs space.
- Split the clock and details into separate colours, hide optional reading titles, or shake to switch your selected reading pair.
- Show one main activity and a smaller secondary summary.
- See a song's title, artist and available artwork while listening.
- Choose which activities appear and put them in your preferred order.
- Adjust the clock format, day/night palettes and Small, Medium, Large or Follow Pebble text. Phone settings group layout, colours, activities and help, with a preview and one Save action.
- Enjoy animated clock layouts, transitions between tracks and support for Timeline Peek.

Try it with Timer: start a countdown, then return to Dynamic to see the time remaining. These ongoing updates are called Activities. Timer still runs the countdown and handles the alarm; open Timer to pause, resume or finish it. Open Activity can add an optional button shortcut back to the app.

Shared readings and activities use the official Pebble phone app's plugin support, source permissions and a connected phone. Music and notification integration requires Android. Notifications are optional; message text is opt-in.

Free. For Pebble Time 2 and Round 2. No account, advertisements or analytics.
