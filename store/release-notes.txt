Dynamic 1.0.0

First stable preparation for Pebble Time 2 and Round 2.

Choose Columns, Stacked rows or Four when idle. The compact four-reading grid returns to the selected pair during an activity or Timeline Peek. Split the clock and detail colours, hide optional titles, or enable shake switching. Complete percentages, including 100%, stay together.

Phone settings now have four category screens, a compact expandable preview and one Save/Discard bar. Day/night palette editing is separate from the active appearance.

Compatible Activities include music, optional notifications and Timer countdowns. Timer owns its countdown controls and alarm; source sharing needs Pebble plugin support and a connected phone.
